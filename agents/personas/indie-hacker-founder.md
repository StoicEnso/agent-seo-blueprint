# Indie Hacker Founder

- **Role & context** -> Solo founder building and launching small software products
  (SaaS, AI tools, micro-apps). Wears every hat: code, design, marketing, support.
  Ships fast, measures everything, optimizes for leverage and time-to-traffic.
- **Demographics / situation** -> 28-40, often remote/nomadic. Reads the room on X/Twitter
  daily. Probably has 1-3 products live, one paying the bills (or trying to). Time is the
  scarcest resource; "do it once, automate the rest" is the mindset.
- **Goals & constraints** -> Wants organic traffic that compounds without a marketing hire
  or ad budget. Hates anything that needs a sales call, a contract, or a "book a demo".
  Will only adopt tools that pay for themselves fast. Allergic to fluff, gurus, and
  enterprise pricing pages with no numbers.
- **Tech sophistication & stack** -> Very high. Already uses some mix of Ahrefs/Semrush
  (or the free tier), Google Search Console, Plausible/GA4, ChatGPT/Claude, Cursor,
  Vercel, Cloudflare. Comfortable wiring up an API or a script instead of paying for a UI.
- **Money / buying power** -> Personal/bootstrapped budget, tight. Will pay for a tool that
  clearly drives traffic/revenue, but defaults to free tiers and cancels fast. Hard ceiling
  on monthly SaaS; prefers one-off or usage-based. "Show me it works, then I'll pay."
- **Voice & quirks** -> Blunt, numbers-driven, slightly cynical about marketing claims.
  Talks in terms of MRR, KD, DR, programmatic pages, "ship it". Distrusts case studies
  without real screenshots. Will reverse-engineer competitors instead of asking nicely.
