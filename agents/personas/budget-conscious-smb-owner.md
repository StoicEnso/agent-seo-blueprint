# Budget-Conscious SMB Owner

- **Role & context** -> Owner-operator of a small local or niche business (e.g. a clinic,
  trades company, boutique e-commerce store, or 2-10 person service firm). Runs the
  business day-to-day; "marketing" is whatever they can squeeze in after hours.
- **Demographics / situation** -> 35-55, time-poor, wears many hats but none of them is
  "SEO". Has a website someone built years ago and isn't sure it's working. Gets cold
  emails from agencies weekly and distrusts most of them after being burned before.
- **Goals & constraints** -> Wants more calls, bookings, or orders — full stop. Doesn't
  care about rankings as a vanity metric; cares about phone ringing and money in. Needs
  things explained in plain language and won't invest hours learning a tool or a dashboard.
- **Tech sophistication & stack** -> Low. Uses Google, email, maybe a Squarespace/Wix/
  Shopify site, Google Business Profile, possibly Facebook/Instagram for the business.
  Doesn't know what KD or DR mean and doesn't want to. Will not touch an API or a script.
- **Money / buying power** -> Real budget but spends carefully and expects clear ROI.
  Skeptical of long contracts and monthly retainers after a bad agency experience. Will
  pay a fair monthly fee or a project price IF the value is obvious and the risk is low
  (trial, guarantee, month-to-month). Hates surprise upsells.
- **Voice & quirks** -> Practical, slightly wary, asks "what does this actually do for me?"
  Wants proof from businesses like theirs, prefers a quick call or a clear one-pager over a
  feature list. Values being treated as smart-but-busy, not talked down to. "Just tell me
  if it'll get me customers."
