# Tibo's eleven growth lessons: evidence-led intake

Source: https://x.com/tibo_maker/status/2119780865131548954 (9 November 2026; reviewed 10 November).

## Scope and evidence

The complete long-form post contains eleven numbered items and one image. This document is an original adaptation, not an endorsement, a vendor integration or independent verification of the author's results. Bazzly and Outrank are source-mentioned products, not required dependencies. Existing tools and approved access are sufficient for a pilot; do not buy a subscription or invoke paid APIs from this document.

The attached image visibly presents 1.06M Google generative-feature impressions and a separate ordinary-search panel with 806K clicks, 20M impressions, 4% CTR and average position 10.2. Its property identity, provenance, export, access scope and causal history are not established. Treat these as source-image observations only, not verified first-party analytics. Never infer AI clicks, AI CTR, AI conversions or cross-provider visibility from that image. The source also reports about $8K generated through Revid affiliates in thirty days. Gross versus net basis, attribution, refunds, commissions and independent verification are unknown.

The broad proposition that mention frequency alone determines AI recommendations is unverified. Do not turn it into a ranking formula, a mention quota, a guaranteed source list or permission to manufacture consensus. A commercial author describing its own tools is not an independent experiment.

## Minimum pilot contract

Copy the eleven-row CSV into the existing project workspace; do not create another workspace silently. Add a dated `research/<date>_tibo-pilot.json` record containing:

- `owner`, `business`, `audience`, `lesson_ids`, `question_evidence`, `current_page`, `product_truth`;
- `hypothesis`, `one_primary_change`, `primary_metric`, `baseline`, `comparison`, `start_at`, `end_at`;
- `success_threshold`, `failure_threshold`, `kill_condition`, `guardrails`, `cost_cap`, `approval_state`;
- `exact_diff_or_draft`, `destination`, `source_evidence`, `receipt`, `outcome`, `decision`, `limitations`.

A useful first scope is three to ten already evidenced questions, one primary change and at most one primary plus two supporting surfaces. This is a bounded study design, not a content or mention quota. Do not test all eleven tactics at once.

Keep public social research read-only. Account creation, posting, review requests, outreach, affiliate changes, publication, production changes, vendor purchases, paid calls and new schedules require the existing exact approval and cost controls. This review grants none of them. Record a live receipt separately from the business outcome after any approved action.

## Measurement and stop rules

1. Preserve ordinary Google Search, Google generative-feature impressions and each answer provider as separate datasets. Missing access or hidden prompts remain unknown, not zero.
2. Use dated literal buyer questions and a fixed prompt-panel version. Provider variability and repeat context must be stated. Citation observations are not causal attribution.
3. Track the relevant product handoff: qualified visit -> useful action -> activation -> retained or paid outcome. Do not substitute page count, mentions, raw review count, screenshots or source-reported revenue for these outcomes.
4. Stop for misleading claims, privacy exposure, platform-policy conflict, poor tool accuracy, negative unit economics or exceeded cost/time limits. Close as `adopt | revise | reject | inconclusive` with evidence. No unattended expansion follows a saved draft.
