# Skill improvement audit - vendor-owned commercial-query citations

**Date:** 2026-08-26

## Source

- Alex Groberman post: https://x.com/alexgroberman/status/2092607970274378041
- A dated copy of the full post was archived in the private research vault.
- All four attached screenshots were inspected as dated observations.

## Evidence checked

- SigNoz and Honeycomb currently publish first-party New Relic alternative pages.
- Search Engine Land reports a Rankscale-powered analysis of almost 8,000 unique citations across 57 diverse queries and names Thinkific, LearnWorlds, Monday.com, Pipedrive, SE Ranking, and HP as vendor-blog examples.
- Ahrefs reports correlations across 75,000 brands: YouTube mentions around 0.737, branded web mentions 0.66-0.71, and site page count around 0.194. The study explicitly states that correlation is not causation.
- The post's current Google and ChatGPT screenshots were preserved as dated observations. They were not treated as stable rankings or provider-wide proof.

## Accepted

1. Add source ownership as an explicit dimension in commercial-query citation audits.
2. Freeze a small high-intent query panel before producing content.
3. Build only pages supported by demand, live format, product truth, distinct buyer value, and current evidence.
4. Require vendor disclosure, common criteria, checked dates, limitations, and losing cases.
5. Retest comparable provider/surface/query/locale/account scopes.
6. Keep Search, Google AI impressions, provider citations, referrals, conversions, and revenue separate.

## Rejected or narrowed

- `10` and `60` content pieces: vendor package counts, not research-backed quotas.
- `3 DR50+ backlinks`: no proof of relevance, citation, traffic, or sales.
- clean HTML, H2 questions, TLDR blocks, lists, and freshness: useful editorial features, not proven universal citation factors.
- `train AI systems`, `citation engine`, and `own the category in 6-12 months`: causal or forecast language not supported by the supplied evidence.
- self-ranking pages: allowed only with clear ownership, fair criteria, current sources, and visible tradeoffs.
