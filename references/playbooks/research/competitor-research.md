# Competitor Research for a New Niche — Can You Outrank Them?

**What it is.** Once a keyword passes volume/KD/intent checks, inspect the sites currently ranking to confirm the competition is beatable — and mine their pages for LSI/keyword ideas you hadn't thought of. The headline insight: a high *domain* authority site can still have a weak *page* (URL rating) for the specific keyword, and that page is winnable.

**When to use.**
- A candidate keyword looks good and you need to confirm the SERP is actually beatable.
- You want to expand your keyword list with terms competitors already rank for.
- Before building, to set realistic expectations on how many backlinks you'll need.

**Method.**
1. **List the ranking competitors.** In Ahrefs, on the keyword, see who ranks in the top results, then open each competitor's site/page.
2. **Check domain authority AND page authority separately.** DA = whole site; PA = the specific ranking URL (Ahrefs = URL rating). The PA is what really matters for that keyword. Look at the referring domains / backlinks pointing at *that exact page*.
3. **Find the weak-page tell.** A competitor can have a high DA but a top-ranking page with only a handful of referring domains (e.g. ~16 links → URL rating ~7). That low page authority means the keyword is winnable with modest backlinks and good content, even though the brand looks intimidating.
4. **Estimate your own gap.** Check where your (planned/existing) page sits and its backlink count to gauge how reachable the top is — e.g. a page with a few hundred backlinks is "doable with some links + quality content."
5. **Mine each competitor's Organic Keywords.** Click a ranking page → Organic Keywords to see every term it ranks for, with positions, KD, and volume. This surfaces LSI variants you missed — e.g. discovering that a `generator` term also ranks for `creator`, `designs`, `designer`, `maker`. Add the useful, low-KD ones to your target list.
6. **Repeat across the top few competitors** and consolidate the new LSI keywords; deduplicate branded terms (ignore competitor brand keywords).

**Decision criteria / heuristics.**
- Judge winnability by **page authority (URL rating / referring domains to the URL)**, not domain authority.
- Top 3 with low PA (single-digit URL rating, few referring domains) = beatable even if DA is high.
- A keyword stays low-KD largely because incumbents' *pages* are weakly linked — verify that's the case.
- Newer modifiers (e.g. `AI` + a category) are low-KD because legacy players rank for the old terms, not the new ones — exploit that.
- Every competitor's Organic Keywords list is free LSI/keyword expansion — always harvest it.

**Example.** Validating `AI tattoo generator`, the top 3 had decent domain authority but the #1 page had only ~16 referring domains (URL rating ~7) — clearly beatable. Opening each competitor's Organic Keywords revealed extra LSI terms (`tattoo creator`, `AI tattoo designs`, `tattoo designer`, `tattoo maker`) worth adding to the same page, all low-KD because they're AI-era variants of older tattoo-design terms.

**Pitfalls.**
- Being scared off by high domain authority while ignoring a weakly-linked top page.
- Not mining competitors' Organic Keywords and missing easy LSI wins.
- Treating competitor branded keywords as opportunities (you can't rank for their brand).
- Assuming low KD without confirming the incumbents' pages are actually weak.
