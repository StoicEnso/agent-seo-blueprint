## Useful additions to the existing workflow

1. **Search before creating.** Check the public product search, signed-in product list and existing tracker/proofs. Claim or update the real existing entry within approval scope; do not create a second listing because the previous session lost its place. Keep product/account identities scoped. Never copy private credentials or tracker data into this public source asset.
2. **Prepare a truthful shared brief.** Use approved product facts, real screenshots and accurate maker representation. Adapt to the current form, not an old guide's character limits. Inspect auto-imported copy before submission. Read back native select values after scrolling or changing them.
3. **Record launch constraints before scheduling.** Capture the earliest acceptable launch date and time zone. Confirm the actual date in the account. A free queue can delay publication for weeks; do not bypass that delay with an unapproved purchase.
4. **Keep operational outcomes distinct.** Record DRAFT, NEEDS_HUMAN, PENDING_REVIEW, QUEUED and SCHEDULED as specific tracker statuses within the existing approval/submission lifecycle. Use LIVE only after public publication is independently verified, with no pending moderation label. Publicly accessible does not mean approved. A confirmation screen does not prove publication. ALREADY_LISTED is an observed prior result, not a new win. DEFERRED_PAID and BLOCKED need a reason and next action.
5. **Require independent evidence per destination.** A partner-distribution checkbox does not prove that partner received or published anything. Do not count the partner as submitted or live until evidence exists on that platform. Save the confirmed state, account identity without secrets, public URL if present, observation date, next action and follow-up date in the existing tracker evidence/notes fields.
6. **Keep side effects separate.** A required website badge can require a source change, commit or production deploy; listing permission alone does not approve those steps. Do not attest that a badge, minimum metric or other eligibility condition exists before checking it. Do not farm comments, invent reviews, solicit upvotes or bypass CAPTCHA/human verification. A source guide grants no permissions. Existing owner-approved authentication may be used only within its allowed scope.

## Examples are hypotheses to recheck, not permanent platform rules

The dated public samples report: Product Hunt scheduling rather than immediate publication; SaaSHub public pages labelled Pending approval; Uneed pending-launch account limits; Fazier eligibility/badge/community conditions; PitchWall imported-copy corrections and a long review queue; TinyLaunch scheduling plus separate upsell dismissals. These are LaunchRepo observations, not independently replayed submissions. Recheck the current destination-owned requirements before use. Do not automate a brittle sequence from these examples.

## Success measure

Keep the existing requirements for destination fit, current submission route, cost/moderation, public output, indexability and actual outbound-link behavior. Measure verified listings and attributable useful referrals, not nominal directory count, claimed domain rating or badges. Unknown stays unknown. No listing, account, purchase, outreach or production action is authorized by this intake.
