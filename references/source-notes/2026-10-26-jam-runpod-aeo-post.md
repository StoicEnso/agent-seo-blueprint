## Full post

> Runpod, a $325M company, increased monthly new paying customers 300% in just 90 days using AI search.
>
> here’s the genius AEO system behind it:
>
> 1. They went from tracking 50 prompts to 300 almost overnight
>
> -> The prompts were split by buyer persona, funnel stage and niche technical use case instead of just tracking broad category searches
>
> -> Their buyers included DevOps engineers, AI builders, researchers and platform teams comparing Runpod against AWS, CoreWeave and Google Cloud
>
> 2. ChatGPT became the channel they prioritized
>
> -> Around 98% of Runpod’s AI referral traffic was coming from ChatGPT
>
> -> So instead of treating every AI engine equally, they focused their monitoring and optimization around the channel already sending real buyers
>
> 3. Their audit found a technical problem most teams would have missed
>
> -> Several Runpod articles were only partially indexed
>
> -> AI could see the title, but not properly access the body content
>
> -> They fixed metadata, restructured the pages and corrected formatting before producing more content
>
> 4 They built prompts around extremely specific technical buying questions
>
> -> “How do serverless GPU platforms compare to traditional cloud infrastructure?”
>
> -> “What’s the best way to manage GPU provisioning and auto scaling for AI workloads?”
>
> -> The goal was to track the questions their actual ICP asks, not generic high-volume keywords
>
> 5. They used the prompts that were already working to decide what to publish next
>
> -> Runpod reviewed which questions consistently surfaced the brand
>
> -> Those prompts became new content titles, angles and topics
>
> 6. Then they exported the entire dataset
>
> -> Prompt performance was filtered by topic, buyer persona and stage of the customer journey
>
> -> Instead of a marketer guessing what article to write next, the content roadmap came directly from gaps in AI search
>
> 7 They paired the prompt data with deeper research
>
> -> Runpod used ChatGPT Deep Research to investigate trending themes, audience questions and gaps in existing content before producing the page
>
> 8. They reverse engineered the sources AI was already using
>
> -> Runpod tracked which owned pages, competitor sites and third-party domains kept getting cited
>
> -> This exposed both content opportunities and external sites where competitors had stronger visibility
>
> 9. Publishing became a feedback loop
>
> -> Find a high-value prompt
>
> -> Research why Runpod is winning or losing it
>
> -> Publish or fix the relevant page
>
> -> Monitor whether Runpod starts appearing more often
>
> -> Double down on the prompts that move
>
> 10. They brought content production in-house
>
> -> Once prompt data and source data were feeding the strategy, Runpod no longer needed to outsource the entire research/content process
>
> -> The team could produce around the exact questions and visibility gaps they were seeing in real time
>
> 11. They cut the cost of their AI search stack by more than 80%
>
> -> Their tooling cost dropped from roughly $3,000/month to $500/month while prompt coverage expanded from 50 to 300
>
> 12. Then it turned into actual customers
>
> -> ~28,000 visitors from ChatGPT
>
> -> ~2,100 conversions
>
> -> 8% conversion rate
>
> -> ~40 new customers per day from ChatGPT
>
> -> roughly a 2-week close cycle
>
> within 90 days, Runpod was bringing in 4x more new paying customers every month.
>
> they didn’t treat AEO like “publish more blogs.”
>
> they built a feedback loop around what buyers ask, what AI recommends, which sources it trusts and what actually turns into customers.
>
> a $325M company turned ChatGPT into one of its top acquisition channels.
