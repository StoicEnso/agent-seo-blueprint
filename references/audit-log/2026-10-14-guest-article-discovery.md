## Bounded live pilot

We screened 15 product/publisher pairs across 12 publishers for three distinct audiences: homeowner landscape concepts, customs-document preparation and GB carrier-licence evidence. Product-specific notes and pitch drafts stay outside the repository. The public template uses only illustrative example.org data.

Current first-party contribution rules and relevant actual samples were inspected for Garden Culture, GardenRant and CargoPlanning. These support four product/publisher route records, **not acceptance**, ready authors, publication or a placed link. We did not meet a three-verified-routes-per-product target and did not pad the queue to imply otherwise. Other destinations stayed conditional or watchlist; a general contact form alone did not count as an outside-contributor programme.

The method exposed material differences:

- Garden Culture: https://gardenculturemagazine.com/write-for-us/ asks for original sourced writing; rejects sponsored posts, paid links and article links from which the writer profits. Inspected sample: https://gardenculturemagazine.com/guide-to-terrace-gardening/. This can fit neutral advice, not a commercial-link request.
- GardenRant: https://gardenrant.com/pitch-an-article-idea-to-gardenrant asks for roughly 400–1,000 opinionated words and 3–5 photos, with no PR talk and no how-to. Sample: https://gardenrant.com/2026/04/counting-the-ecological-contributions-of-non-native-plants.html. Its image file-size wording is inconsistent; confirm limits before an approved send.
- CargoPlanning: https://cargoplanning.com/en/write-for-us requires 500–1,500 original words, an author name and a suitable headline image. It permits an author-bio website link, rejects affiliate/sales-page/unrelated links, and can edit, refuse or remove work. Sample: https://cargoplanning.com/en/blog/article/6/how-to-select-the-perfect-freight-broker-for-your-business. It is a vendor-owned blog; the sample is from February 2022 and the inspected index's latest posts are March 2025. Route verification does not establish strong editorial quality or an active response rate.
- LASN: https://landscapearchitect.com/research/editorial/editorial-submissions.php requires a genuine landscape-architect project, design/team details, site plan and 8–20 photos. A generic software article is not a substitute. Project/asset eligibility remained conditional.
- Other alleged guideline pages returned 404/403, redirected to a generic blog or lacked a contribution programme. Successful HTTP status and a contact link were not enough. A submission form with repetitive low-value coverage was not promoted into the priority queue.

All placement fees remain **unknown**. No absent fee became “free.” No author credential, product performance, traffic, domain metric, acceptance or link treatment was inferred.

## What changed

Added one narrowly scoped authority playbook, an illustrative ledger, an offline query generator/evidence checker, regression tests and routing from authority, content and monitoring. The checker validates saved structure, timestamps, first-party URL relationships, hashes and quoted excerpts. It does not read semantics, assess publisher quality, verify claims, resolve public-suffix ownership, collect website data or authorise outreach.

No accounts, messages, forms, payments, memberships, article publication, schedules, website edits or merges were performed by the pilot. Send/publication receipts belong in a separate approval-bound outcome log. No ranking, indexing, referral or revenue effect was tested.
