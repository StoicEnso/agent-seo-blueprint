# Source audit — weekly GSC action queue

- Source: Mila Chervenkova post, https://x.com/milachervenkova/status/2109259283523043469. It reports a single exact-match-domain outcome and suggests a Monday GSC task list. The reported 30-to-100+ clicks/day movement is not controlled, causal, or transferable proof.
- Primary method limits: Google Search Analytics query documentation, https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/searchanalytics/query — final-only comparable data, row limits, and scope/aggregation matter.
- Quality constraint: Google helpful-content guidance, https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content — do not change dates or add/remove content merely to appear fresh.
- Adopted: bounded, evidence-backed review queue. Rejected: automatic GSC connection, fixed Monday schedule, new-page selection from impressions alone, automatic CMS writes, or any ranking/traffic/revenue guarantee.
