- Linkdr belongs to the separately reviewed vendor material. The interview does not establish it as Railway’s supplier.
- Revenue, company size and reported outcomes remain interview claims, not audited results or proof that a named tactic caused growth.
- Current Railway product access can differ from the interview’s shorthand. The playbook retains its first-party documentation correction.
- A chapter link, paragraph number, summary or short quote does not authorise publishing, buying links, outreach, data transfer or deployment.
