## Primary evidence and corrections

Graphite is the agency describing its own Webflow partnership: an agency-authored
case report, NOT independent verification or access to raw analytics. The primary
page describes historical 2025 work, not a fresh October 2026 experiment. A sign-up
means a **free Webflow account**, not a paid customer, qualified lead or revenue.

- 168→983 is approximately 485.1% relative growth for template-page LLM signups
  Q1→Q2, not established as all-site growth, unique paid accounts or causal proof.
- 33%→64%share of voice is 31 percentage points/about 93.9% relative growth.
  Graphite defines its own exact-term-match answer metric; it is not a universal
  citation/market-share measure. Raw samples and full repeated-panel metadata are absent.
- 4%→8% is the **share of total signups** coming from LLMs, not necessarily twice
  the absolute number of LLM signups. Its denominator can change.
- 24% vs 4% is a 6x reported signup conversion-rate comparison with **non-brand SEO**,
  not all Google traffic or paid conversion. The template cohort and aggregate CVR
  are not established as identical. Do not multiply 983 by 24% or forecast revenue.
- Ten long-tail questions, 38%→100% SoV and 70% improvement after three weeks are
  scoped case claims. Do not extrapolate them to all buyer questions/providers.
- Reddit 10% of ChatGPT **citations** and YouTube 32% of Perplexity **responses** have
  different denominators/providers. Neither justifies a source/channel quota.
- The 1,500-page initiative and 100-query Reddit programme are case scale, not a
  minimum. The 98% sentiment/135% views/12% members/145% posts+comments are vendor
  reports, not independently verified acquisitions or proof of community causality.

## Raw and adjusted changes are not interchangeable

The primary page explicitly distinguishes raw and control-adjusted results:

| Variant/metric | Reported means | Raw relative growth | Reported control-adjusted growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Table of contents, AI visits |119→127|about 6.7%|60%|
| Table of contents, clicks |191→220|about 15.2%|23%|
| Question headings, AI visits |41→70|about 70.7%|58%|

The control means, URL-level raw data, variance, random-assignment evidence and
analysis code are not supplied, so the adjusted effects and p-values cannot be
independently reproduced. Never calculate 60% from 119→127 alone. The primary
attributes part of the ToC effect to a decline in controls. A low p-value is not
an effect size, proof of absence of confounding, a probability the hypothesis is
true, or a guaranteed repeatable gain.

The case describes 10 test URLs matched to 10 controls with similar traffic and
impressions over a **five-week design**, with four-week outcome statements and
significant results after week 4. Preserve that distinction, rather than silently
choosing a duration. Reported p-values: questions 0.001, ToC 0.002; not reproduced.
Matched observational controls improve interpretation but do not establish
randomization or prove the individual changes caused signup/revenue growth.
