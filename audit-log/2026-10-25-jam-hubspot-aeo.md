# Source audit: jam's HubSpot AEO system

Checked: 25 October 2026. Scope: source preservation and an evidence-gated strategy
extension in both Agent SEO repositories. No project deployment or tool purchase.

## Sources

- Original: https://x.com/wespreadjam/status/2114021961450033430 (24 October 2026).
- [Complete saved post](../references/source-notes/2026-10-25-jam-hubspot-aeo-post.md).
- First-party: https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/hubspot-aeo-case-study (read 25 October).
- Original image is preserved beside the post. It shows one annotated ChatGPT-style
  recommendation screenshot. It does not expose a reproducible full prompt history,
  model/account/locale/sample protocol or independent evidence of the growth figures.

## Cross-check and limits

HubSpot's own page confirms that it REPORTS: eight product containers plus an
overall brand container and feature views; four buying stages; case-study-derived
industry pages with human review; 92% cited and 49% visibility lift; industry
comparisons with 642% citation and 58% mention increases; glossary citation share
+60%; product-page citations +56%; publisher partnerships at hundreds/~1,000-page
scale; weekly Reddit gap research; France 33.5% and Germany 17.1% mention rates;
and overall 433% more citations, 1,850% more qualified AI leads and 3x conversion.
The overall figures appear in the page's result summary, which a readability-only
extract can omit. The full public HTML/text was checked, not only a search snippet.

This establishes first-party self-report support, NOT independent verification,
causal proof, a replicable forecast, or evidence that the same method will work for
another company. No underlying lead-level data, full prompt panel, absolute lead
counts, stable denominators or experimental controls were independently audited.
The Q1 2025–Q1 2026 interval and $13.5 billion valuation/headline in jam's post were
not established by the inspected case-study page. These remain source-only claims.
The primary page states 3x versus other sources; jam says up to 3X traditional search.
Do not silently equate those comparator definitions.

Schema and content changes were bundled. The case study does not isolate FAQ or
Breadcrumb schema as the cause of citation growth. It does not grant special Google
AI eligibility, guaranteed FAQ rich results, or proof of model-training inclusion.
A publisher being cited is not proof it endorses the brand or is available for hire.

## Decision

Adopt product/feature/intent grouping, actual-citation source mapping, evidence-led
industry/comparison/glossary/product-page briefs, and gap-specific routing through
existing workflows. Adapt scale to one real site and one bounded pilot. Preserve
human review, truthful product claims, customer rights, community disclosure,
editorial independence, current source custody and exact-action approvals.

Do not adopt mass page quotas, manufactured case studies or community influence,
hidden sponsorship, a mandatory HubSpot/XFunnel subscription, universal GEO scores,
expected uplift percentages, automatic outreach, or new recurring jobs.

## Integration

The shared guide maps all 12 source points. The gap-action template and full-pack
brief use the existing visibility/citation/content/authority/monitoring workflows.
Repository source updates do not install or activate this skill in any agent.
