## Source claim

The creator says SEO, answer-engine optimization, and social have converged; customer-question data can drive 10–30 founder-led videos; LinkedIn Pulse may rank for difficult Google and large-language-model queries; and one search-led idea can perform across social and answer engines.

## Evidence state

The video provides no query list, comparison cohort, account history, publication sample, Google-update evidence, attribution window, traffic or conversion data, or provider-specific prompt/citation record. The cross-surface outcome claims are therefore unverified creator hypotheses.

## Accepted method

- derive questions from dated customer/search evidence;
- check the owned answer and cannibalization risk first;
- pilot 3–10 questions rather than impose a content quota;
- pair each answer with a real expert, proof asset, claim boundary, and limitations;
- live-check every candidate search/social surface;
- adapt one primary asset for no more than two supporting surfaces;
- keep search, native, referral, answer-provider, lead, and conversion evidence separate;
- require explicit approval before each external publication.

## Rejected claims and tactics

- no claim that a Google update caused convergence;
- no guarantee that LinkedIn, social posts, or videos will rank;
- no guarantee that search demand predicts social performance;
- no promise that one asset will appear across all answer engines;
- no 10–30-video quota, host-authority exploitation, cross-platform duplication, fabricated expertise, or automated publishing.

No external content was published and no outreach was sent during this repository update.
