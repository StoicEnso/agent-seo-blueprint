## What are these posts?
They are channel updates, not a requirement to upload a video: text, images/GIFs, polls, quizzes, or shared video posts. A public channel post has a `/post/<id>` URL. YouTube calls this feature **Posts**, historically **Community posts**. The channel Posts tab/shelf and possible Home/Subscriptions distribution are distinct from Google rankings. Current help also says image posts may appear in the Shorts feed. None of those placements is guaranteed.

Do not confuse these with the separate **Communities** feature, where subscribers can start discussions. Official help says Community-only posts are not shown to viewers on the public Posts tab. Do not substitute one surface for the other in an SEO test.
