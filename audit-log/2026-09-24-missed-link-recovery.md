## One bounded test if a suitable site exists

1. Select a directory/page family with verifiable, licensed or first-party data, a real search task and distinct value per leaf. Establish canonical rules, a hard page-count cap and a small initial cohort before release.
2. Inventory only owned, permitted URLs. Check key pages for crawlable, contextual inbound links; review any automated suggestion against the existing `assets/internal-link-ledger.csv` fields (source, destination, natural anchor, reader reason, canonical status).
3. Record a dated Search Console baseline and later matched windows for indexation, page/query clicks and qualified actions. Log other changes and stop or consolidate thin/duplicate pages. Do not infer causation from a six-day screenshot or buy indexing to force the outcome.
4. Consider a platform-native video only when the observed buyer query warrants a visual demonstration. Review rights and publication separately; measure YouTube reach, search clicks, AI mentions, referrals and conversions as different series.

No URL inventory was submitted to a third party; no indexing service, bulk link insertion, outreach, content publication or spending was undertaken. Source stories and product capacity are hypotheses, not validated benchmarks.
