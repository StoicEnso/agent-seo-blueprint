## What the primary supports, and what it does not

Scrunch attributes the result to a customer story and named Runpod growth lead.
It reports 50 to 300 tracked prompts, persona/funnel/niche coverage, about 98%
referral share for ChatGPT, partial article-body rendering/index visibility,
metadata/structure/formatting changes, export requirements, in-house production,
$3,000 to $500 monthly platform spend, and fourfold monthly new paying customers.
No reproducible raw analytics, account cohort, complete prompt panel, affected
URLs, matched comparison or causal control is included.

- The 98% wording leaves referral taxonomy/denominator ambiguous. Do not claim it
  is all company traffic or prescribe the same engine mix to another business.
- 2,100 / approximately 28,000 = 7.5%, consistent with a rounded 8%; the count
  called conversions is not established as unique new paid accounts. Keep that
  separate from approximately 40 new customers per day and a two-week close
  cycle. Reporting windows and event definitions are not given well enough to
  multiply or reconcile those figures. Do not manufacture a 90-day total.
- 4x is +300% only for the same baseline metric and comparable period. This is
  not fourfold total revenue, profit, all-channel customers or our expected gain.
- $3,000 to $500 is about 83.3% lower **platform spend**. It does not establish
  all-in cost savings after labor, collection, model/research and maintenance.
  The historical figures are not verified current subscription prices.
- 50 to 300 is 6x prompt coverage, not 6x visibility, quality or conversion. A
  changed evaluation panel cannot support an unqualified trend comparison.
- The $325M valuation, two exact sample questions and named ChatGPT Deep Research
  step in the post are not established by the inspected primary. Keep them
  source-only. Required data export is not proof of an installed live collector.
- The image is one annotated answer that also recommends a different product for
  a different need. It is not proof of stable rank #1 or accurate current GPU rates.
- Title/body visibility is a reported diagnosis, not raw proof of the exact
  mechanism or that metadata changes alone caused the customer outcome.

## Existing coverage and review dependency

The main repositories already contain the question panel, provider-separated
answer metrics, citation-source inventory, feedback workflow, approval queue,
content QA and monitoring. The open HubSpot reviews (public #54 / Full #47) add
the gap-to-action guide and source-complete site review. This change is stacked
on those exact review branches, not a second copy of their workflow or an implied
merge. All twelve new source points are reconciled in
[the source map](../assets/aeo-runpod-source-map.csv). The repository review is
completed; the separate project measurement template is deliberately not run.

The missing delta tightens body-delivery evidence, provider allocation from real
referrals, stable cohorts despite panel growth, customer/lag definitions and
comparable total costs. See the [existing guide extension](../references/hubspot-aeo-gap-to-action.md#runpod-acquisition-evidence-extension)
and [inert review record](../assets/aeo-acquisition-review.json).
No new playbook, monitor, generic dashboard, provider integration or mandatory
vendor purchase is introduced. No site evaluation, customer result, live panel,
site change, outreach, production release or schedule was executed.
