## Intake and safe test design

Use `assets/parasite-seo-idea-pilots.csv` with the existing hosted-publishing workflow. All 11 rows default to DRAFT_RESEARCH_ONLY, with no public URL, acquired link or outcome. Do not merge an example’s source-page link with a project’s actual backlink.

Keep each site separate. Define a real reader job, original evidence, relationship disclosure, exact destination, lawful rights and a measurable referral outcome. One local prototype or source screenshot is not a live pilot. Require exact publication approval before any account, article, Sheet, deck, store listing, job or repository-site write. Buying a site, paying a fee and changing redirects need separate scoped approval. A pilot request is not an unlimited platform batch.

## Claim review

1. Next-year content: useful forward planning may be tested; do not invent future tests or claim the first page always wins.
2. arXiv/Zenodo: actual research only. No disguised promotional paper, bogus authorship or fabricated dataset.
3. Chrome extension: require an independently useful extension, permissions/security review and current developer-account/fee eligibility. A store listing is not ranking proof.
4. LinkedIn article: concise answer-first copy is a testable editorial choice; neither 330 words nor the universal #1-source claim is established.
5. Jobs: a real vacancy and authorised hiring owner are required. Reject fake vacancies and manufactured branded-search campaigns.
6. Aged website: require due diligence, actual content/business continuity, budget and a lawful migration. Reject expired-domain abuse and assumed ranking transfer.
7. Spreadsheet: original useful criteria, privacy check and transparent connected-brand disclosure. Do not rig a supposed independent table with the sponsor always first.
8. LinkedIn slug: accurate title and natural URL. No title bait-and-switch or guaranteed keyword-slug ranking; immutable behaviour needs current verification.
9. Gamma: one audience-useful deck at most per exact approval. No ten-deck quota or duplicate doorway pages; sharing/indexing/link attributes remain separate.
10. GitHub: a genuine educational/open-source resource, not a cloned commercial SaaS landing page. Current Pages limits prohibit free hosting primarily for an online business or commercial SaaS.
11. Quora: a current relevant question, genuine answer, expertise and disclosure. Low answer count is not low competition or proof of traffic; never drop URL spam.
