## Priority for a bounded, owner-reviewed trial

1. **Highest fit:** audit one existing high-impression/low-click or near-page-one page, verify intent with customer language, improve the answer and internal links, record a 28-day baseline and a 28/56-day follow-up. This extends existing Search Console and content-opportunity work; no new mass-content loop is needed.
2. **Second:** review one About page for factual gaps and buyer questions. Draft copy from actual company facts, product evidence and consented customer research. Do not fill a template with invented data.
3. **Third:** prototype one free tool only where it solves a real repeated task better than current options. Measure task completion, relevant search visits and qualified product actions separately.
4. **Optional editorial outreach:** screen a small number of genuinely relevant publishers from a directory; prepare drafts for human review, not an automated email waterfall. Explicit owner approval is still required to contact anyone.
5. **Not adopted:** host-reputation abuse, unmoderated public pages, mass Google Docs, link-first guest-post exchanges, and unattended publication. Their apparent speed carries policy and quality risk.

## Independent policy check

Google's [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) define link spam, scaled-content abuse and site-reputation abuse. Google's [people-first guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) recommends original value and accurate authorship; it explicitly says E-E-A-T is not a specific ranking factor. These official pages constrain the social posts' claims. Source claims, reach figures and ranking effects are **not** adopted as verified facts.
