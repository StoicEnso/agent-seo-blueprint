## Accepted edits

1. Added a statistics-page link-earning playbook that treats the post as a hypothesis, not a ranking or backlink promise.
2. Added a source/method/outcome brief with explicit approval and maintenance fields.
3. Preserved the useful mechanism: current-year title, stable yearless URL, one auditable original number, visible dates, honest schema, quotable formatting, contextual links, and periodic refresh.
4. Narrowed the footer advice. A footer link is optional and allowed only for a genuine site-wide research resource.
5. Added stop conditions for copied facts, thin annual clones, cannibalization, privacy/licensing issues, unsupported schema, and misleading outreach.

## Rejected or deferred

- **“Rank without backlinks in 24 hours.”** Rejected as an outcome promise.
- **“Small sites rank fast for these keywords.”** Kept as a targeting hypothesis until a target SERP and owned result set support it.
- **“Journalists will link.”** Rejected as a guarantee. Measure real citing pages and referral traffic.
- **Mandatory footer placement.** Rejected. Contextual links with a reader reason are the default.
- **Scaled annual page generation.** Rejected. One stable canonical page must be refreshed in place.
