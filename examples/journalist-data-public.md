Eight traffic-area rows plus one separately labelled Great Britain total. Each year's regional sum equals the national total: 682 then 783. North Eastern counts rose from 74 to 166 (+124.3%), while Western counts fell from 124 to 103 (−16.9%); the national change is +14.8%. These are reproducible calculations, not exclusive new raw data or a causal discovery.

**Limitations:** these are completed goods-vehicle public inquiries for non-compliance, not unique firms, all operators, inspections, convictions or a regional probability of offending. No operator-population adjustment is applied. Do not sum different regulatory actions: more than one action can occur per operator. The report warns about annual comparability and manual compilation; preserve that caution. Do not call the largest count/change the least safe region. Two annual observations do not establish a long-term trend.
