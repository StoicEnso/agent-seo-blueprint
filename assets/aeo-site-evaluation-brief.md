## Evidence pack contents

- `SOURCE.md`: full original post, not only a summary; original image/locator.
- `CLAIM-AUDIT.md`: first-party reported figures versus independently observed facts.
- `REPORT.md`: concise executive result, site-specific decisions and top 3–5 actions.
- `APPLICABILITY.csv`: all 12 points, including already-covered/rejected ones.
- `PAGES.csv`: current URL/feature, intent, audience, evidence, overlap, recommended diff.
- `PANEL.csv`: 3–10 source-grounded questions in one justified group with stable versions;
  validate using the existing prompt-panel schema. Mark proposed questions not_run.
- `EVIDENCE/`: dated raw permitted answers/citations and relevant current page captures.
  Reuse existing evidence when fit. Do not run a paid/live panel without its authority.
- `MISSING-DATA.md`: unavailable property/export/provider, exact need, next safe action;
  preserve unknowns, periods, filters, units and partial coverage.
- `BRIEFS.md`: exact first useful page/diff, proof, internal link, CTA, reviewer, gates;
  fair comparisons, real customer proof only, no thin industry or locale permutations.
- `MEASUREMENT.md`: provider-specific mentions/citations, Google web, Bing All,
  Bing Web+Chat, Google AI impressions, referrals, leads, paid conversions and revenue
  kept distinct. Definitions, denominators, baseline/retest periods, costs and kill rules.
- `MANIFEST.json`: path, byte size and SHA-256 for every delivered file.
- Checked ZIP or complete Markdown pack for the owner, with private data kept private.
