# Outreach email — DRAFT (review before sending)

> ⚠️ **This is a DRAFT for the user to review and send manually. It is NEVER auto-sent.**
> The authority-and-links workflow drafts outreach into the workspace; a human reads,
> edits, and sends it. No email is dispatched without explicit confirmation. Personalize
> every send — templated-looking mail gets ignored or marked spam, which is the opposite
> of what link building needs.

## Send context (not part of the email)

- **Target site / page:** {{TARGET_URL}}
- **Recipient name & role:** {{RECIPIENT_NAME}} — {{ROLE}}
- **Why this target:** {{the specific reason — they linked to a competitor, broken link,
  they cover this topic, resource-page fit, etc.}}
- **The asset we want linked:** {{OUR_URL}} — {{why it genuinely deserves the link}}
- **Angle:** {{link-stealing / broken-link / resource-page / guest mention / HARO follow-up}}

---

## Subject

{{SUBJECT — short, specific, no clickbait. e.g. "Broken link on your {{TOPIC}} resource page"}}

## Body

Hi {{RECIPIENT_NAME}},

{{Open with one genuine, specific line proving you actually read their work — reference
the exact page/post by name. No generic "love your blog".}}

{{State the reason for reaching out in one or two sentences. Be direct and useful:
the broken link you found, the gap their resource has, or the relevant thing you made.}}

{{Make the ask clear and low-friction, and frame it as a benefit to THEIR readers — not
a favor to you. Link to {{OUR_URL}} and say in one line why it's worth their readers' time.}}

{{Optional: one line of credibility or proof — only if true and brief.}}

No worries at all if it's not a fit. Thanks for your time either way.

Best,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
{{YOUR_SITE / ROLE}}

---

## Pre-send checklist (for the human reviewer)

- [ ] Recipient name and the page reference are correct and specific (not a mail-merge slip)
- [ ] The reason to link is genuinely valuable to THEIR audience, not just to us
- [ ] Our linked asset actually delivers on the promise (no bait-and-switch)
- [ ] Tone is human and personalized — would not read as a mass template
- [ ] No false claims, fake compliments, or pressure tactics
- [ ] You (the user) have chosen to send this — it is not, and will not be, auto-sent
